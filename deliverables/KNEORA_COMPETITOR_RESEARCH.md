# Kneora: competitor research and storefront direction

Research date: September 10, 2026. Target store: `d0mcjv-p0.myshopify.com`.

## Scope and evidence

Retrieved published page content directly from the four supplied URLs using web retrieval. This is a content-and-structure audit, not a visual screenshot or interaction audit: browser inspection was blocked by an unavailable security-policy check. Exact typography, spacing, mobile behavior, sticky behavior, checkout flows, and image-only wording were not verified. Repeated content in extraction can represent responsive duplicates rather than visible repetition. Offers are a dated snapshot, not independently verified promises.

The user chose research and prompt first. No theme ZIP or store changes are included in this phase. Product-image generation is paused.

## 1. IM8 Health

**Observed:** Promotional strip combines 30% savings, welcome gifts, shipping, and a 90-day guarantee. The hero places ambassador content, a rating with review count, purchase CTA, and testing/certification markers close together. Subsequent material presents routines, product systems, per-serving economics, and expert/ambassador stories. Navigation exposes science, ingredients, standards, reviews, and FAQs.

**Useful mechanism:** Layer the purchase benefit, reassurance, and supporting evidence instead of asking one badge to establish trust. Give buyers a deeper destination for product questions. A system/bundle can feel more complete than an isolated item.

**Kneora application:** One calm promotional strip, an organized purchase panel, clear included-items information, and an accessible product-details destination. Use real evidence and real people when available; do not borrow celebrity relationships, certifications, review totals, or supplement economics.

Source: [IM8 homepage](https://im8health.com/).

## 2. Grüns

**Observed:** The page leads with an occasion-based promotion, shipping/guarantee reassurance, ratings, a clear product promise, and a savings CTA. Product selection exposes flavor, sugar level, quantity, and purchase frequency. Selected plans explain price, delivery cadence, shipping, cancellation, and guarantee together. Further content makes use simple, opens nutrition information, and explains third-party testing. Headline savings and displayed plan prices differ by configuration.

**Useful mechanism:** Make options understandable at the moment of selection, with nearby explanations of precisely what changes. Turn instructions and detailed facts into accessible supporting content.

**Kneora application:** Two clearly differentiated offer cards with full contents, actual prices, and availability. Update every price display together. Include an expandable specifications panel and straightforward usage media. Do not add a hardware subscription or carry over supplement-specific testing claims.

Source: [Grüns homepage](https://gruns.co/).

## 3. Rythm Health

**Observed:** The opening combines a simple at-home proposition, rating/result-volume indicators, 25% promotion, $79/$59.25 pricing, and an explicit shipping-and-tax qualification. Nearby content lists a 30-day guarantee and eligibility/accreditation markers. Later sections include named endorsements, service inclusions, dispatch/result timing, subscription flexibility, and explanations of laboratory ownership and sample handling. A CTA still mentions $79 alongside the discounted price.

**Useful mechanism:** Explain the practical process and who stands behind it. Place transaction details beside the offer, then supply deeper operational evidence further down.

**Kneora application:** Show what arrives, how the dual-knee device fits, how the controls work, and how to contact support. Separate dispatch time from delivery time. Keep CTA pricing synchronized. Laboratory credentials, HSA/FSA eligibility, outcome percentages, and testing turnaround do not transfer to Kneora.

Source: [Rythm homepage](https://rythmhealth.com/).

## 4. LEVIDE

**Observed:** Despite the URL slug, the page displays Knee Companion 2.0. The opening contains a countdown, gallery, crossed-out/current pricing ($329.99/$189.99), specifications, offer selection, quantity, and purchase CTA. It advertises gel, brace, and e-book bonuses. Supporting accordions state a two-year warranty, 90-day returns, free worldwide shipping, 1–3 working-day processing, and 7–14-day shipping. Social proof includes Facebook review imagery and persona-based testimonials. Later content covers upgrades, comparison, and claimed research. Embedded product links show different prices ($199.99/from $99.99). Some delivery/timer fields remain unresolved in extraction.

**Useful mechanism:** Bundle contents and purchase reassurance are concrete; customer stories speak to recognizable circumstances.

**Kneora application:** Adopt the device-plus-aftercare offer structure, compact information accordions, and relevant customer contexts. Do not copy its customer statements, efficacy figures, hardware specifications, charity relationship, or price inconsistencies. Kneora policies must be its own.

Source: [LEVIDE supplied product page](https://levidecupp.com/products/levide%E2%84%A2-red-light-therapy-panel).

## Kneora decisions

### Brand and audience

Kneora is practical premium knee-care equipment for contractors, flooring installers, tilers, carpenters, plumbers, mechanics, landscapers, and other labor workers. Speak respectfully about long days and wanting an uncomplicated routine at home. Include women in trades as well as men. Avoid macho caricatures, fear-based messaging, a fake founder history, or suggestions that the device is protective jobsite equipment.

Visual direction: workwear navy `#20364B`, chalk white `#FAFCFD`, steel grey `#DCE3E8`, muted slate `#526775`, and amber `#D5A442`. Use navy for primary buttons and amber sparingly for offer emphasis; maintain readable contrast. Firm, approachable typography and precise alignment should carry the identity. Build an original identity instead of copying any competitor's assets or distinctive expression.

### Product truth

One rigid dual-knee unit, **360 × 242 × 160 mm**, with two separate knee openings, dark interior padding, and a central divider. White bridge-shaped shell, grey control panel, blue-lit display. User states heat, massage, red light, and blue light. Supplier evidence is still required for mode counts, light wavelengths, power source, temperature settings, session duration, suitability, and therapeutic claims. The latest front-view image overrides inaccurate generated lifestyle images.

### Offer structure

| Choice | Customer-facing contents | Commercial status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single Kneora Massager | One dual-knee massager; clarify that one device serves both knees | User-required option; price and box contents unconfirmed |
| Kneora Knee Massager + After Care Healing Gel | One dual-knee massager plus one gel unit | User-required option; gel name, quantity, formula, directions, compatibility and price need confirmation |

Suggested positioning: call the second choice the **After-work care set** in supporting copy. Emphasize the extra included item and genuine bundle savings once established. Use “Bundle option” until actual savings justify “Better value.” Do not call it “Most popular” without sales evidence.

Proposed commercial policy for evaluation: a simple bundle discount, free shipping if margins support it, and a clearly explained trial/return policy. The reference sites suggest testing a 30–90-day window; this is not an adopted Kneora guarantee. Do not invent reference prices or add a brace/e-book without a real deliverable.

The user calls the gel “healing gel”; this is a working product label, not evidence that it heals. Do not instruct customers to combine gel with heat/light/device contact until manufacturer compatibility and instructions are confirmed.

### Fixed product-page order

Desktop: carousel left; headline/subheadline, four benefits, offer/CTA, risk reversal, and purchase-objection FAQs right. Then full-width social proof, followed by education. This follows the supplied outline. Mobile must preserve content priority in a single readable column; do not compress the entire opening section into one viewport.

Education should include product construction, actual operation, correct dual-knee positioning, suitability, supported comparisons, practical expectations, and further FAQs. Expert/authority and results sections need real evidence before customer-facing publication.

### Trust details to implement

- Correct dimensions and a clear both-knees view establish product understanding before persuasion.
- Selected offer visibly changes its border, radio state, included-items summary, price, and cart payload.
- One-time purchase is stated clearly. No surprise recurring gel purchase.
- Real shipping and returns information remains reachable near the purchase control.
- FAQs address fit, use, contents, gel, shipping, and support.
- Reviews link to full reviews only when available. No invented totals, copied testimonials, stock people presented as customers, or fabricated verification badges.
- Show warranty duration, support hours and response expectations only when supplied.
- Repeat the purchase opportunity after education, carrying the same selection and price.
- No perpetual countdown or random stock counter. Show a dated promotion only when it has a real configured end.
