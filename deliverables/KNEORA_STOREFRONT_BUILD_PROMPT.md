# Kneora storefront build prompt

Use the prompt below with the attached research notes, product-page outline and front-view product reference.

---

You are the creative director, ecommerce strategist, and senior Shopify theme developer for **Kneora**. Build a complete, distinctive, production-quality Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme for **d0mcjv-p0.myshopify.com**. Deliver an uploadable theme ZIP, editable source, setup instructions, and an honest validation report. The result must function as a Shopify storefront, not merely resemble one in a static mockup or externally hosted React application.

## 1. Business objective and audience

Kneora sells a dual-knee massager for people whose work puts demands on their knees: contractors, tilers, flooring installers, carpenters, plumbers, mechanics, landscapers, and other labor workers. The core setting is an uncomplicated at-home routine after the workday. Include both men and women in the audience. Speak with practical confidence and respect for skilled work.

The product page itself is the landing page for Meta and TikTok advertising. It must explain the product quickly to a first-time visitor, answer buying objections, make the offer easy to understand, and support a confident purchase. Design for mobile traffic from the beginning. Conversion is an objective to measure, not a result to claim in advance.

Build a premium consumer equipment brand: reliable, clear, considered, and approachable. Avoid a bargain gadget-store tone, hospital aesthetic, bodybuilding aesthetic, exaggerated toughness, generic wellness language, or startup-template appearance.

## 3. Product facts and imagery

The device measures **360 × 242 × 160 mm**. It is ONE wide unit for BOTH knees at the same time, not a single-knee wrap or two separate devices. It has a satin-white bridge-shaped housing, two separate open-bottom knee channels, dark padded side walls, a central divider between knees, a grey control panel, and a black blue-lit display.

User-stated functions: heat, massage, red light, and blue light. Do not invent wavelengths, power ratings, charging behavior, battery life, temperature ranges, mode counts, weight, session duration, automatic shutoff, medical certification, waterproofing, or fitting range. Do not infer a strap system from another product.

Preserve product proportions and correct dual-knee use in every asset. Discard inaccurate earlier generated imagery. Do not publish screenshot play icons, store UI, heat arrows, transparent anatomy, or marketplace badges. Provide editable image/video slots while final media is pending. In theme-editor preview only, use purposeful labeled placeholders; live pages must not show broken images, fake product renders, or development notes. Document any missing launch assets separately.

Plan gallery roles: clean overall product, front view showing both knee openings, seated dual-knee use, controls detail, dimension diagram based on confirmed dimensions, and bundle contents. An absent role should not create an empty thumbnail. Photography should show ordinary after-work environments and realistic workwear; avoid suggesting the massager is used while kneeling at a jobsite.

## 4. Brand system

Create an original Kneora wordmark with a firm, approachable typographic character. Avoid a generic medical cross, shield, lightning bolt, or construction clipart. Keep the product unbranded in images unless approved brand application exists.

Use this initial palette, refining contrast while keeping its character:

- Workwear navy `#20364B`: primary identity, headings and purchase buttons.
- Chalk white `#FAFCFD`: main page canvas.
- Steel grey `#DCE3E8`: secondary surfaces and dividers.
- Muted slate `#526775`: supporting type and icons.
- Amber `#D5A442`: small offer highlights and selected-detail accents with dark text.

Use a sturdy humanist or industrial sans-serif for headings and an exceptionally legible companion for body copy. Explore Barlow Semi Condensed for major headlines and Source Sans 3 for body text, or well-matched Shopify font-picker alternatives. Use properly licensed, locally served or Shopify-hosted fonts, no more than two families. Body text should generally be 16–18px; avoid tiny policy text and widely tracked uppercase labels.

Typography, product scale, and composition should create hierarchy. Use modest 6–10px radii on interactive containers, disciplined spacing and restrained borders. Do not put every paragraph inside an identical rounded card. Avoid decorative gradients, glass panels, floating emoji, random number badges, infinite marquees, generic bento layouts, excessive pill buttons, fake dashboards, and repetitive scroll-reveal effects.

Make the store feel designed around a real piece of equipment: precise specification rows, honest material closeups, substantial offer cards, clear instructions, and well-framed lifestyle photography. Maintain a balanced rhythm of product presentation, editorial explanation, comparison, and purchase opportunities.

## 5. Required product-page architecture

Follow the supplied outline closely. Do not insert a large campaign hero before the product-buying section.

### A. Site chrome

Use one compact announcement bar for a real configured offer or useful factual message. No auto-resetting countdown. A compact header contains the Kneora wordmark, Shop, How it works, Our story, Support, and cart. Optional Reviews navigation appears only with genuine review content. Mobile navigation must be easily operable and restore focus on close.

### B. Opening two-column purchase section

At desktop, use approximately 52% media / 48% purchase content in a 1200–1320px centered container, with a deliberate 40–64px gap where space permits. Align both columns at the top. Keep the product large and fully visible.

**Left:** one main image/video carousel with a thumbnail rail immediately underneath. Include zoom, clear active thumbnail state, keyboard access and meaningful alt text. Do not autoplay sound or force continuous motion. Sticky media is acceptable only when it does not interfere with reading or short screens.

**Right, in this exact order:**

1. Headline and subheadline. Suggested original direction: **“The workday ends. Make room for knee care.”** Supporting copy: “A dual-knee massager with heat, massage, and red and blue light for your at-home routine.” Add a compact real rating link near the heading only when review data exists.
2. Four concise benefit bullets: two about the practical customer experience, then two about verified product functions. Begin with convenience and both-knee use rather than an unproven pain-reduction promise. Suggested themes: an after-work routine at home; one unit for both knees; heat and massage; red and blue light. Refine for clarity without inventing outcomes.
3. Offer selector and purchase CTA, as specified below.
4. Risk-reversal information directly beneath the CTA. Include actual returns, warranty and shipping details when supplied; keep essential qualifications readable and link to full policies.
5. Compact purchase-objection FAQs: both-knee fit, box contents, operation, gel choice, and delivery/returns. Use accessible accordions, with answers based on actual documentation.

The diagram's fold line describes hierarchy. Do not shrink the type, media, or touch targets to force this entire section into one mobile viewport. On mobile use gallery → headline → benefits → offer → reassurance → FAQs.

### C. Social proof immediately after the purchase section

Create a three-column desktop section for real customer photos and concise stories that address different objections, then an accessible mobile layout. Use a real trade or job context only if supplied by that customer. Reviews must not be invented, copied from competitors, or assigned to stock/AI portraits. Verified-buyer labels require actual verification.

Provide review-app block support and an editable testimonial section. If genuine reviews are unavailable, hide the section on the live storefront and preserve its intended position in the editor; use clearly labeled preview placeholders only in design mode. Do not substitute invented statistics to fill the gap.

### D. Education, following the outline

Use varied, coordinated modules in this order:

- Qualified explanation or authority module, enabled only with verified expert/source material.
- Construction and materials: annotated real product imagery and sourced specifications.
- How it works: explain confirmed controls/functions in plain language, distinguishing product features from medical outcome claims.
- How to use it: a short visual sequence showing correct seated dual-knee placement. Populate timing, intensity, power and cleaning instructions only from the manufacturer.
- Who it is for: connect to after-work contexts without asserting universal suitability.
- Comparison: a factual comparison of product configurations or documented alternatives. Include a clear comparison basis; do not invent competitor weaknesses.
- Technology details: structured expandable information for the buyer who wants specificity.
- Expectations and routine: practical guidance grounded in real instructions. No fixed recovery timeline, guaranteed healing, or clinical percentages without appropriate product-specific evidence.
- Further FAQs: fit limitations, power, care, gel details, delivery, returns, warranty and support.

Finish with a restrained purchase reminder using the same selected offer and actual price, then the footer. Repeat useful information deliberately rather than padding the page with repetitive claims.

## 6. Offer and bundle experience

Provide exactly these initial choices:

**Single Kneora Massager**
- One device, designed for both knees together.
- List actual included items when confirmed.

**Kneora Knee Massager + After Care Healing Gel**
- One dual-knee device plus one gel unit.
- Supporting label: “After-work care set.”
- Confirm gel size, formula, instructions, availability and product naming before launch. “Healing gel” is the user's working label, not substantiation for a healing claim. Never advise applying gel under the heated device without verified compatibility.

Render these as two large accessible radio-style offer cards. Each shows actual price, contents, concise description and selected state. A bundle photograph appears only if accurate media is available. Use “Bundle option” by default; show a savings badge only when a real comparison price establishes the saving. Use “Most popular” only if sales data supports it. Do not manufacture urgency, stock counts, crossed-out prices, free gifts, warranty terms or savings.

Select the single device by default unless a variant-specific URL requests another valid selection. Make the bundle appealing through clarity and actual value, not by obscuring the lower-priced choice. Allow the user to switch without losing quantity. State one-time purchase; no subscription is requested.

The primary action is “Add to cart” with a loading state, success announcement and actionable error message. Keep offer price, comparison price, availability, included-items summary, sticky mobile bar and cart line items synchronized. Format money with Shopify's store currency. Honor sold-out/unavailable configurations and deep links.

Use real catalog variants or a real bundle product as appropriate to the store's fulfillment model. If device and gel require component inventory tracking, configure Shopify-compatible bundle inventory rather than disguising the addition in cart properties. Never claim a gel has been added when only the device variant was purchased. Record required catalog configuration in setup instructions; a theme ZIP cannot create inventory or discount rules by itself.

Create coordinated templates and editable sections for:

- Main product page: the fixed outline above, tuned for ad traffic.

- About: original positioning around practical after-work care; do not invent a founder, manufacturing location, years in business or customer history.
- How it works/product guide: sourced controls, dimensions, fit and instructions.
- Support/contact: working contact form, FAQ access and configured support details.
- Shipping, returns, warranty and privacy/terms destinations using merchant-supplied policies; do not fabricate legal text as settled policy.
- Cart page and cart drawer: actual variant contents, quantities, removal, price updates, empty state, errors and standard checkout handoff.
- Search, 404 and password page with coherent styling and useful navigation.

Use the footer for a small brand statement, shop/help links, real policy links and contact access. Newsletter capture must work if enabled, with clear signup intent. Do not add obstructive entry popups.

## 8. Fine interaction details

- A mobile sticky purchase bar appears once the main purchase control leaves view, summarizes the selected option, and avoids covering content or the device safe area.
- Offer radio controls have visible keyboard focus and a selected indicator beyond color.
- Changing an offer announces relevant price/availability updates without interrupting navigation.
- Accordions have useful questions, accessible state, and no layout jumps caused by delayed scripts.
- Media reserves dimensions to prevent layout shifts. The first important image loads promptly; later media loads lazily.
- The cart shows the right bundle name and contents, supports quantity changes, and reflects server-confirmed totals.
- Delivery information distinguishes processing from transit. Do not show a fabricated arrival date or a permanent “loading” label.
- Payment icons derive from actual accepted payment methods, not an arbitrary badge collection.
- Sticky elements, support buttons and cookie interfaces must not overlap on small screens.
- Buttons, menus, forms, search and checkout links must work; no decorative dead controls.
- Support reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, keyboard operation, form labels, error announcements and roughly 44px touch targets.
- Use restrained, purposeful transitions for user-triggered changes. Do not animate every section on scroll.

## 9. Shopify implementation and export

Build native Liquid, JSON templates, sections, blocks, snippets, theme settings, locale files, CSS and lightweight JavaScript using current Shopify documentation. Use standard theme directories and a valid theme layout with required Shopify content hooks. Do not deliver a Vite/Next.js ZIP and call it a Shopify theme.

Expose text, colors, typography, media, offer product/variant selection, FAQs, policy links and section visibility in the theme editor. Keep product data and prices connected to Shopify. Support app blocks for reviews and other necessary merchant integrations. Use locale-aware routes, translated interface strings, responsive Shopify images, and proper product forms with a basic non-JavaScript purchase path.

Use real product structured data and canonical metadata; never add fake review schema. Preserve campaign parameters where relevant without sending personal or health information in analytics. Use Shopify-supported customer-event and consent integrations for Meta/TikTok tracking; avoid duplicate pixel installation. Document tracking setup rather than embedding arbitrary identifiers.

Do not require an external paid site host or runtime to operate the theme. Put theme directories at the ZIP root. Exclude secrets, node_modules, research files, local caches, test credentials and development artifacts from the upload ZIP. Deliver documentation alongside it. Do not publish or replace the live theme without explicit launch approval.

## 10. Validation and deliverables

Before declaring the theme ready:

1. Run Shopify Theme Check and validate JSON/templates/section schemas; correct errors and explain unresolved warnings.
2. Test with actual preview products and both offers: default selection, variant URL, selection changes, quantity, sold-out state, cart addition, removal and checkout handoff. Do not place a paid order.
3. Test menu, gallery, zoom, accordions, forms, search, cart drawer, sticky purchase controls, policy links and app-block behavior.
4. Inspect real screenshots at representative mobile and desktop widths, including 375px, 390px, 768px and 1440px. Check text wrapping, clipping, product geometry, overlapping controls, and large-font usability.
5. Check keyboard and screen-reader essentials, visible focus, contrast, reduced motion and loading performance. Report measurements only if actually measured.
6. Confirm unsupported claims, empty evidence sections, placeholder customer stories and fake offers are absent from the live experience.
7. Upload-test the package as an unpublished theme in the authorized store when access permits. If access is unavailable, say that installation and store-based testing remain unverified.

Deliver: `kneora-theme.zip`, full editable source, a theme setup guide, a catalog/offer mapping sheet, an asset/content checklist, and a validation report listing what was actually tested. Distinguish **uploadable**, **configured**, and **launch-ready**. Missing catalog data, final product photos, gel instructions, policies or store access must not be hidden behind a claim that the store is ready to sell.

Start by reviewing the supplied outline and research, summarize the visual direction, then implement the storefront to this brief. Keep design choices deliberate, copy specific to skilled workers, the product representation accurate, and the purchase flow straightforward.
