## Build checklist derived from the structure image

The following translates the supplied diagram into practical product-page features. Knee-wrap examples are adaptations for this store, not additional facts extracted from the video.

| Feature | Application to the website product page |
| --- | --- |
| Product media carousel | Include clear product views, a wrap-in-use image, detail views of actual materials/components, and a short fitting/use demonstration. |
| Outcome-led introduction | Pair a clear headline and subheadline with four concise benefits: two customer outcomes and two explanations grounded in the product's verified mechanism. |
| Complete purchase offer | Keep actual price, included items, any required options, and the purchase CTA together so the visitor can understand the offer before buying. |
| Reassurance beside the CTA | Explain the actual returns/guarantee terms and make shipping information easy to find. Specific policies remain to be determined. |
| Early objection FAQs | Answer the highest-priority purchase questions near the offer; candidate topics include fit/sizing, adjusting the wrap, comfort, usage, and returns. Confirm answers against the real product. |
| Relevant social proof | Use authentic customer photos and reviews that address specific concerns. Follow the image's three-example layout when suitable material exists. |
| Visual product education | Explain how it works and show how to put it on and adjust it through concise steps and a GIF or video. |
| Product suitability and comparison | Explain who the product is intended for, its actual materials/technology, and supported differences from alternatives. |
| Expectations and follow-through | Include supported usage guidance, realistic expectations, and additional FAQs further down the page. |

Preserve the selected order: **product media and purchase section → social proof → education and further FAQs**.
